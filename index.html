Before you try it

  • A prototype, not financial advice. Categories, totals and forecasts are worked out automatically and can be wrong. Check anything important against your bank's own statement.
  • Your statements stay on your device. Files are read inside this browser and saved only here. Nothing is uploaded or sent to a server. The PDF reader and fonts load from public code libraries, which never see your files.
  • Use your own computer. Anyone using this browser profile can see what you import. On a shared or work computer, remove everything afterwards with Your data, then Delete my ledger.
  • Your bank details are kept with the statements. Original statements are stored so you can reopen them, and they include whatever your bank prints, such as account numbers and your address. A backup copy holds every transaction, so keep it as private as a bank statement.
  • Not affiliated with any bank. Revolut, American Express and Monzo are named only to describe the statement formats it reads; they have not reviewed or endorsed it.
